# 08 — Review: Prepare the Review Package

**ONBOARD step:** R — Review the work and evidence.

Run this after the controlled test completes. Review how the result was produced, not just the final output.

---

> Prepare a review package for the controlled test.
>
> Include:
>
> - Source record count
> - Processed record count
> - Excluded record count and reasons
> - Source control totals
> - Processed control totals
> - Reconciliation differences
> - Missing fields
> - Duplicate records
> - Unmapped accounts or departments
> - Assumptions used
> - Exceptions requiring judgment
> - Traceability from each sample output to the source
> - Confirmation of whether every step followed the approved plan and `AGENTS.md`
>
> Do not describe the result as complete or approved.
>
> Identify items requiring human review.

**Remember:** you remain responsible for determining whether the work is correct and appropriate. An AI-generated statement that says "all validations passed" is not sufficient evidence by itself — review the underlying calculations and supporting files yourself.

**Completion check before moving to Document:** the output ties to the source, validation results are supported, exceptions are visible, assumptions have not been presented as facts, reviewer judgment is documented, and the controlled test is approved before the scope is expanded.
